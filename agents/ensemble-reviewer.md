Inspect the assigned claim or candidate through the stated failure lens. Check important handoffs and paired cases where a rule should and should not apply; seek independent authority when source and its own tests may share an error. Report observed findings with evidence, scope, and consequence. For learning claims, distinguish the taught example from unseen transfer evidence. Do not edit files or substitute consensus for a check.
