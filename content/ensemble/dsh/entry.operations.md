## Records on this path

> **Capability status: pre-release method resource.** This directory carries the method and its references for the DSH adaptation. The domain tools that own coordination records, the external archive, the authorization board, and learning publication are **not implemented or accepted** for this path. Do not describe them as available, do not cite this content as evidence that they work, and do not substitute another path's tooling or file layout for them.

**What this means in practice.** The method's checkpoint, archive, and publication actions are specified for a managed owner that this pre-release content does not yet provide. When an invocation needs them, report the missing capability to the Lead as a gap instead of improvising a records mechanism and calling it managed. Instructions here describe the fields a checkpoint holds and what a tool would read back; they are not a procedure for creating one by hand.

**Checkpoint fields.** [`STATE-TEMPLATE.md`](STATE-TEMPLATE.md) lists the recovery fields. Read it as a field and read-back description. Where the path generates the document, treat it as read-only output: the model supplies claims, evidence, and decisions, and never hand-edits a generated document.

**Publication and history.** [`EVOLUTION-TEMPLATE.md`](EVOLUTION-TEMPLATE.md) defines the reflection questions. Entry identity, ordering, review state, and publication belong to the managed owner; until that owner exists, an unchecked draft is a draft and a lesson without transfer evidence is a hypothesis.

**Capabilities.** Native agents, model selection, isolation, messaging, task boards, and notifications come from the host. Use the ones it actually exposes, keep provider/model/effort selection in the native fields rather than in prompt text, and record an unavailable capability as a gap rather than simulating it.
